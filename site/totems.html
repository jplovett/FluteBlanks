Totems & fetishes

Hand-carved totems that sit on the nest area of your flute. Since they are hand carved, no two are exactly alike.

  • For the small key of high C flute: the small bear (smooth or rough), buffalo or roadrunner.
  • For all of our other flutes: the larger bear (smooth or rough), eagle, buffalo (standing or resting), coyote, kokopelli, or bear with fish.

The bases are designed to fit the nest area on our flutes and may not fit the nest area of other flutes.

Each totem is $7.00.
