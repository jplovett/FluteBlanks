Shipping and handling

  • Standard shipping in the continental US is USPS or UPS ground. FedEx is not available.
  • There is a minimum charge of $18.00 for UPS ground. USPS charges vary by region.
  • For example, one finished flute ships UPS for $18.00, and three finished flutes ship UPS for a total of $18.00.
  • UPS cannot deliver to PO boxes.
  • Orders received before 1 PM Eastern ship the same day. Orders received after 1 PM Eastern go out the next business day.
  • We do not ship on weekends.
  • Orders outside the US are billed for the actual shipping charges. All charges, including shipping, must be paid in U.S. dollars before shipment.
